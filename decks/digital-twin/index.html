More data than ever.
Less clarity than ever.

Founder Pain 01
Truth lives in 7 tools
Notion, Drive, Airtable, your CRM, Slack, the heads of your co-founders. None of them speak to each other. Performance numbers in one tool, pricing logic in another, customer truth nowhere.
Founder Pain 02
Infrastructure paid, nobody uses it
A VPS sits idle. Automation tools billed monthly with no clear owner. The website needs migrating. Without a frame, you don't know what should run on what, or in what order to fix it.
Founder Pain 03
No memory between decisions
The same strategic question gets re-debated every quarter. Hire vs no-hire, pricing variant, ICP cut. Nobody logs the reasoning. Every quarter is week one again.
The bottleneck is not data. It is the unified frame for thinking through it.

What this is about · the team problem

N roles, dozens of processes, one company brain.
But nobody uses it.

Team Pain 01
Knowledge lives in N heads
Founders + heads of function + operators. Processes get mapped in Miro or Notion every six months. But nobody can find the right doc in the moment they need it, so the work happens by tribal memory instead of by reference.
Team Pain 02
Founder A says X, founder B says Y, team remembers Z
Every person has their own version of the truth. Setup drift grows with every new hire. The same tool gets re-configured because nobody knows it was already configured. Trust quietly erodes.
Team Pain 03
Without a brain, onboarding is 4 weeks of "let me update you"
A new hire starts at zero. Senior people lose hours per week getting them up to speed. The hidden cost: the senior people are also slower at their own work, because their context lives in their head, not in a system.
The cost is not software. It is the distributed context every role rebuilds from scratch every day.

Layer 01
Founder Twin (you + co-founders)

Each layer is useful alone. Together they compound: better decisions, on shared context, every day.

Layer 1 · how the Founder Twin thinks

5 layers.
Every strategic decision runs through them, in this order.

Layer 01
Sales + Delivery Reality
Pipeline, wons, active accounts, customer concentration, retention signal, velocity. Hard facts before opinions.
Layer 02
Financial Dashboard
Cashflow, margin per account, budget remaining, recurring vs project revenue, runway. The decision must survive the P&L.
Layer 03
Quarterly OKRs
The rocks you committed to this quarter. A decision that breaks the sprint needs a louder reason than habit.
Layer 04
Strategy Lock
ICP, positioning, pricing, service tiers, your vivid vision. The frozen decisions you already paid for.
Layer 05
Mentor Avatars
Pattern-match against 3-5 of the best operators in your category. What would the smarter peer say to this?
A decision that contradicts 3 or more layers triggers a stop-and-think alert. The twin refuses to push it through.

Layer 2 in action · how team and brain exchange context

The team feeds the brain.
The brain feeds back.

What the team puts in (push)
01
Pull Requests against your-org/company-brain
Account Manager files a customer performance snapshot. COO updates voice rules. CEO logs a pricing decision. Head of Delivery extends customer map. The brain versions itself. Every change reviewable, revertable, audit-trailed via GitHub.
02
VPS cron jobs pull automatically
sales_bridge.py hourly. marketplace_pull.py every 6h against your data APIs. pipeline_daily.py Mon-Fri 18:30. The team feeds the brain by working, not by uploading.
03
Weekly cowork session live in the brain
Founders + advisor online, brain open. Last week reviewed, this week's risks surfaced, one new twin output drafted live. The session commits back to the brain as a decision log with a 30-day outcome review.
What the brain gives back (pull)
01
Frame-checked answers in Slack or meeting
"What does the brain say about Customer X?" Twin pulls all 5 layers, returns a frame-stamped recommendation. 20 seconds. Exactly where the team already works. No new tool to open.
02
Drafts in your founder voice
Customer replies, LinkedIn posts, website updates. Pattern-checked against the voice library before they leave the brain. Founder reviews, marketing ships.
03
Risk flags and Daily Brief 07:00
Pipeline stagnation, customer concentration, churn signal, OKR drift, stress markers before a decision push. The brain surfaces them in the morning brief on Slack, not in the next quarterly review.
The brain is not a tool the team opens. It is the layer behind the tools the team already uses (Slack, storage, Notion, your CRM).

What runs under the hood

All your data.
Pulled live. Always current.

Script What it does
sales_bridge.py CRM live: opportunities, wons, lost, pipeline, leads, lost reasons. On-demand snapshot with short cache.
data_pull.py Product / channel APIs: usage, performance, retention signal, engagement velocity per account.
financial_pull.py Live finance sheet pull: P&L per customer, margin per account, headcount, recurring tracking, runway.
pipeline_daily.py Daily snapshot + diff vs yesterday + risk flags (stage stagnation, overdue, pipeline drops, churn score spikes).
icp_match.py ICP classification P1/P2/P3/OUT on new leads from outbound pipeline. Plug into your existing email or sales sequence.
stress_detector.py Pre-decision stress score across 4 trigger categories. Refuses to push on a red score. Pause template instead.
All Python. All in git. ~200 lines per pull. No vendor. No no-code tool in the critical path. Cron on your VPS for the schedules.
